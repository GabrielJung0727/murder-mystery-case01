## 1. 음악

### 공통 스타일

모든 곡 프롬프트 뒤에 붙이세요.

```
1920s colonial Korea, Gyeongseong, period cinematic score, Korean traditional instruments blended with a small Western chamber orchestra, instrumental, no vocals unless stated
```

- 메뉴·조사·재판 곡은 **2~3분, 끝이 처음과 이어지는 루프**로 고르세요. 프롤로그·엔딩 곡은 루프가 아니어도 됩니다.
- 노래가 섞인 결과물은 빼 주세요 (CV-OST08 의 합창만 예외).

### CV-OST01 · 타이틀 ★★★

비 내리는 경성 거리. 무겁고 비장하지만 아름답게.

```
Somber, dignified main theme for a historical courtroom drama. Rain ambience, slow solo haegeum melody over low cello and soft piano, distant gayageum plucks, builds to a restrained string swell with a deep buk drum, then falls back. Minor key, 70 BPM, noble sorrow, unbreakable resolve, seamless loop.
```

### CV-OST02 · 프롤로그 — 총성 ★★★

말발굽 소리에서 시작해 '탕' 직전에 멈춘다. 루프 아님, 60~90초.

```
Tense cinematic prologue cue. Begins with low strings and a ticking clock, horse hooves rhythm in percussion, rising tension with tremolo violins and a heartbeat bass drum, sudden silence at the climax for a single gunshot, then a long low drone with scattered piano notes. 60 to 90 seconds, no loop.
```

### CV-OST03 · 증거 배분 ★★★

서류를 넘기며 고르는 시간. 차분하지만 불안하게.

```
Investigative underscore, minimal and curious. Muted pizzicato, soft piano ostinato, typewriter-like woodblock clicks, occasional gayageum harmonics, low tension pad underneath. 90 BPM, quiet, focused, seamless loop.
```

### CV-OST04 · 비밀 대화 ★★

법관석과 피고인석이 따로 속삭이는 시간.

```
Hushed conspiratorial underscore. Breathy daegeum flute, low sustained strings, faint muffled rain behind a window, sparse piano, whispery textures. Dark, intimate, secretive, 65 BPM, seamless loop.
```

### CV-OST05 · 공개 재판 ★★★

검사와 판사가 몰아붙이는 법정.

```
Courtroom confrontation theme. Driving low strings ostinato, staccato brass hits, jing gong accents, janggu rhythm under a march-like snare, stern and oppressive, Japanese colonial court atmosphere, rising pressure. 100 BPM, seamless loop.
```

### CV-OST06 · 최후 질의 · 구형 · 판결 ★★★

마지막 라운드. 숨 막히게.

```
Climactic verdict music. Slow heavy buk drum heartbeats, sustained choir pad without words, solo haegeum crying above dark strings, gavel-like percussion strikes, unbearable suspense that never resolves. 60 BPM, seamless loop.
```

### CV-OST07 · 엔딩 — 희생 ★★★

순국·희생 엔딩(E1, E3, E5). 애도.

```
Mournful elegy. Solo haegeum and piano, slow and tender, gentle strings enter in the second half, memorial for fallen independence fighters, quiet dignity, ends on an unresolved soft chord. 2 minutes, no loop.
```

### CV-OST08 · 엔딩 — 의거 · 해방 ★★

법정 의거·특별사면·서대문형무소 구출 엔딩(E2, E4, E6). 비장한 승리.

```
Heroic bittersweet finale. Starts with a lone daegeum, builds to full orchestra with Korean buk drums, brass fanfare, wordless choir, a hopeful Korean folk-style melody, triumphant but tearful, liberation 1945. 2 to 3 minutes, no loop.
```

### CV-OST09 · 에필로그 ★

엔딩 뒤 공통 맺음말("끝내 누가 총독을 향해…") 아래.

```
Reflective epilogue. Soft piano and haegeum duet, warm strings, sense of history and remembrance, calm and resolute, 90 seconds, no loop.
```
